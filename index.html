FAQ

Everything you want to know before you call.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. The evaluation and estimate are free, with no obligation.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Charlotte?

The City of Charlotte has a tree ordinance that can apply to certain large or protected trees. If you're not sure, we'll help you check before starting the work.

Do you include cleanup and branch removal?

Yes. We chip and haul away plant material as part of the service, unless you'd like to keep the firewood.

Do you handle emergencies for fallen trees?

Yes. If a tree fell on a structure, fence, or roadway, contact us and we'll prioritize based on the situation.

What size trees do you handle?

From small yard trees to large, mature specimens. Tell us the approximate height and site access so we can quote accurately.

What areas do you serve besides Charlotte?

North and South Carolina, roughly within 70 miles of Charlotte — including Concord, Gastonia, Statesville, Rock Hill, Fort Mill, and Lancaster. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.
